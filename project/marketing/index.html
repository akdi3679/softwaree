FAQ

Where is my data?

On your Admin computer. Encrypted backups go to our Cloud (Frankfurt), but we cannot read them.

What if my Admin dies?

Replace it. Settings, Device, Replace. We email you a code. The old one is auto-revoked.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Settings, Data, Export. ZIP with everything.

Is it really open source?

The clients (Admin, User, modules) are MIT/Apache-2.0. The Cloud is proprietary but trivially self-hostable.
