# Launch: Day 1 to Day 7

## Day 1 (launch day)

### Hour 0 - Push the button
- [ ] Publish the blog post
- [ ] Post on socials (Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit r/SelfHosted, Hacker News)
- [ ] Email the waitlist
- [ ] Activate Stripe live mode
- [ ] Set status page to all operational
- [ ] All hands on deck in #launch

### Hour 1-4 - Watch the metrics
- [ ] Signups (target: 5-20)
- [ ] Signup to first project (target: over 60 percent)
- [ ] First project to first backup (target: over 80 percent)
- [ ] Errors in Sentry (target: under 1 percent)
- [ ] Support emails (response within SLA)
- [ ] Twitter mentions

### Hour 4-24 - Triage
- [ ] Triage support emails (1h for urgent)
- [ ] Triage bug reports
- [ ] Post a Day 1 update
- [ ] File issues found during the day

## Day 2-3 - Listen

- [ ] Read every support email carefully
- [ ] Look for patterns: same complaint from 3+ customers, fix it
- [ ] If anything is broken: hot-fix, do not wait
- [ ] Do not add new features yet. Stabilize.

## Day 4-5 - First retrospective

- [ ] Gather the team
- [ ] What worked? What did not?
- [ ] What surprised us?
- [ ] What is blocking customers from getting value?
- [ ] File top 3 fixes for next week

## Day 6-7 - Communicate

- [ ] Send a Week 1 update to customers
- [ ] Post stats on socials (signups, WAP, NPS)
- [ ] Thank the team
- [ ] Plan next week

## What NOT to do in the first week

- Do not add new features
- Do not change pricing
- Do not change terms of service
- Do not launch big marketing campaigns
- Do not speak at conferences
- Do not hire anyone yet

## What to watch for

- Cloud error rate (alert at over 1 percent)
- API latency p99 (alert at over 1s)
- Backup success rate (alert at under 99 percent)
- New signups per day (baseline)
- Conversion to paid (target: 8 percent)
- Support tickets per customer (target: under 1)
- NPS (target: over 40)
- Twitter sentiment (manual scan)

## When to panic

- Cloud is down for over 15 min
- Data loss reported by any customer
- A critical security issue
- A major competitor launches something similar

Otherwise: take a deep breath. We are fine.
