# 1000 - The First Thousand Customers Playbook

> **Status:** Playbook, not a promise. Numbers are targets, not forecasts.
> **Audience:** founders, sales, support

How we get from zero to 1000 active projects without breaking the
product, the ops team, or our sanity.

---

## Phase 0 - First 5 (beta)

Who: hand-picked clinics and labs, known to us personally.

Goal: **find out what breaks.**

- Onboard in person or on a call.
- Sit with the Admin user for the first 3 days.
- Log every confusion, every crash, every "why does it do that."
- Weekly retro.

Exit criteria: 5 customers using it daily for 4 weeks with no P1 incidents.

---

## Phase 1 - First 50

Who: customers referred by Phase 0.

Goal: **make onboarding self-serve.**

- Publish the onboarding wizard.
- Write the first-customer runbook.
- Build the support inbox workflow.
- Instrument: time-to-first-project, time-to-first-sync, drop-off points.

Exit criteria: a new customer can go from payment to a working project
in under 30 minutes without a support ticket.

---

## Phase 2 - First 500

Who: direct outreach, industry forums, referrals.

Goal: **make support scale without hiring linearly.**

- Public docs site.
- Self-serve knowledge base.
- Automated diagnostics (the export_diagnostic_bundle we already built).
- Shared inbox with 4-hour SLA.

Exit criteria: 1 support person handles 100 active projects without
burnout.

---

## Phase 3 - First 1000

Who: all channels.

Goal: **make operations cheap.**

- Regional Cloud instances if latency demands.
- Automated backup restore drills (already have `restore_drill.rs`).
- Metrics-driven alerting (see METRICS.md).
- First hire: an ops engineer.

Exit criteria: 1000 active projects, < 1 full-time ops person per 500
projects, 99.5% uptime over 90 days.

---

## What we do NOT do

- We do not raise prices to chase revenue.
- We do not add modules to hit a roadmap date.
- We do not compromise the local-first model to make support easier.
- We do not sell customer data. Ever. (It does not leave their device.)

---

## What could kill us

Ranked by realism:

1. **Support cost outruns revenue.** Mitigation: self-serve, diagnostics.
2. **A P1 incident damages trust.** Mitigation: honest post-mortems, real DR drills.
3. **A competitor with a hosted EMR undercuts us.** Mitigation: our
   local-first model is a feature, not a limitation, for the right
   customer. Do not chase the wrong customer.
4. **The team burns out.** Mitigation: hire before we need to, document
   everything, automate the boring parts.

---

## The single metric

**Weekly active projects** - a project is active if at least one user
logged in in the last 7 days.

Not MRR. Not signups. Not downloads. If a project is not used, it is
not a customer.
