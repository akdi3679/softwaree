# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue.**

Email: security@product.local

We will acknowledge within 24 hours, triage within 72 hours, and provide
a fix timeline within 7 days. We follow responsible disclosure.

## Scope

In scope: Admin/User Tauri apps, Cloud Hono API, module runtime, backup
encryption, sync protocol, our mesh ACLs.

Out of scope: underlying infrastructure vendors, third-party marketplace
modules (report to their publisher).

## Security advisories

Published at: https://product.local/security/advisories (placeholder)
